# Description

**All activities, words, and directions on the worksheets are in Spanish. There is no English text on the student pages.**

**55 completely new activities, plus 1 bonus activity.** None of them repeat Volume 1, so together the two volumes give you 111 different activities, each at two levels of difficulty.

The activities are highly visual, so students can understand what to do by looking at the pictures and drawing steps, even if they are still developing their Spanish language skills. This makes the resource useful for Spanish classes, dual language and immersion classrooms, Spanish-speaking students, and newcomers.

Drawing helps children develop creativity and fine motor skills, while tracing pictures and words provides additional handwriting practice. These activities help children focus, improve hand-eye coordination, and gradually build confidence in both drawing and writing.

Each activity follows four simple stages:

**1. Sigue los pasos - Follow the Steps.** The picture is broken down into small, easy-to-follow steps. Students can see where to start and what to draw next. This helps them stay focused and gradually become more confident in their drawing skills.

**2. Repasa - Trace.** Students trace the lines and shapes of the completed picture, developing fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination.

**3. Dibuja tú solo - Draw on Your Own.** Students draw the picture on their own, choose their colors, and can add their own details, encouraging independence and creativity.

**4. Repasa y escribe - Trace & Write.** Students first trace the Spanish word and then write it independently, getting additional handwriting practice while reinforcing Spanish vocabulary and spelling.

The resource includes 55 activities plus 1 bonus activity, covering a wide variety of themes: animales, bichos y criaturas pequeñas, animales marinos, fantasía, transporte, deportes y pasatiempos, objetos, naturaleza, and comida. The drawings vary in complexity, allowing teachers to choose activities that best fit their students.

Each activity is provided at two difficulty levels:

**Level 1.** The picture is completed in **4 steps**. At each step, the part already drawn appears in light gray, while the new lines to add appear in black. The drawing area includes a light-gray starting shape, so the student continues the picture and adds the remaining details.

**Level 2.** The same picture is completed in **more steps - from five to eight, depending on the drawing**. Students begin with the simplest shapes and gradually add details. There is no starting shape in the drawing area, so students complete the entire picture independently.

This gives teachers **112 ready-to-print worksheets** at different difficulty levels. Level 1 can be used at the beginning of the school year, with a gradual transition to Level 2 as students become more confident and ready to work more independently.

The two levels also make it easier to work with students at different skill levels without having to find or modify separate activities for each child. The whole class can work on the same picture while the teacher chooses the appropriate level for each student.

No additional prep is required: simply choose a page, print it, and use it in class.

These activities work well for **Spanish classes, dual language and immersion classrooms, Spanish-speaking students, newcomers, morning work, learning centers, independent work, early finishers, and sub plans.**
