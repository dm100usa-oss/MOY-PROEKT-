How Each Activity Works

Each activity takes students through four stages.

1. Follow the steps.
Students see how the drawing is built from simple lines and shapes.

2. Trace.
Students trace the finished drawing, reinforcing the hand movements before drawing on their own.

3. Draw and color.
Students recreate the drawing independently, then color it.

4. Trace and write the word.
Students trace the Spanish word and then write it themselves, practicing handwriting and reinforcing vocabulary at the same time.

Drawing, fine motor practice, handwriting, and Spanish vocabulary all come together in one activity.

For Early Finishers

Once students have drawn the main character or object, they can use the open space around it to keep going.

Around a finished animal, for example, students can add trees, grass, rocks, the sun, clouds, or other characters and turn a single drawing into a scene of their own.

The small themed illustrations and lettering on each page can also be colored. Students who finish the main task early have something meaningful to continue working on independently while other students finish the activity.
