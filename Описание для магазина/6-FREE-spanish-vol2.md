## Title

FREE Spanish Directed Drawing Volume 2 | 9 No Prep Dibujo Dirigido Worksheets | K-2

## Description

This free set includes 9 complete directed drawing activities for Grades K-2, with Spanish vocabulary on every page. They are the same worksheets that appear in the full collection, Volume 2, with nothing shortened and no watermarks.

The set includes one activity from each theme in the book, so you can see the range of drawings and judge whether the format suits your students.

No prep is needed. Choose a page, print it, and use it in class.

**How Each Activity Works**

Each page takes a student through four stages.

1. Follow the steps. Students see how the drawing is built up from simple lines and shapes.

2. Trace. Students trace the finished drawing, reinforcing the hand movements before working on their own.

3. Draw and color. Students recreate the drawing independently, then color it.

4. Trace and write the word. Students trace the Spanish word and then write it themselves, practicing handwriting and reinforcing vocabulary at the same time.

Drawing, fine motor practice, handwriting, and Spanish vocabulary all sit in one activity.

**For Early Finishers**

Once a student has drawn the main character or object, the open space around it can be used to keep going.

Around a finished animal, for example, a student can add trees, grass, rocks, the sun, clouds, or other characters, and turn a single drawing into a scene of their own.

The small themed illustrations and the lettering on each page can also be colored. Students who finish the main task early have somewhere to continue working independently while the rest of the class catches up.
